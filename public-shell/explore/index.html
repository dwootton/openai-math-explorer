How references are compared

Families are compared by the works listed in their manuscripts’ bibliographies. DOI and arXiv aliases are merged, with conservative title-and-author matching for other citations. Each work counts once per family. Widely shared works count less, long lists are normalized, and scores based on very few shared works are reduced.

Open a neighbor’s shared works to see the evidence. This measures shared literature, not whether two proofs use a citation in the same way. Unmatched families sit in a separate row. Map positions are approximate; lines follow the reference ranking.
